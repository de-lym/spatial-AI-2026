    About & limits
      
    A fictional block-based architectural sandbox: a South Block District with lower buildings and a North Tower District with taller, denser ones. All buildings, street names, heights, volumes and numbers are synthetic and invented. Coordinates: local Cartesian x east, y north, z up in metres above one shared ground datum; 1 scene unit = 5 m. navigable = permitted − restricted − buildings − clearance, and it can be disconnected: a drone may not climb over a building if the altitude above lies outside every permitted volume. Obstacle checks are a simplified classroom rule, not certified collision avoidance, and nothing here claims regulatory compliance or real airspace authorization.
