    About & limits
      
    A fictional block-based architectural sandbox: a South Block District with lower buildings and a North Tower District with taller, denser ones. All buildings, street names, heights, land uses, volumes and numbers are synthetic and invented. Coordinates: local Cartesian x east, y north, z up in metres above one shared ground datum; 1 scene unit = 5 m. navigable = (land-use airspace ∩ altitude envelope) − buildings − restricted volumes − clearance; it can be disconnected, and a drone may not climb over a building if that altitude is not permitted. Obstacle and drone-separation checks are simplified classroom rules, not certified collision avoidance. Nothing here claims regulatory compliance, real airspace authorization or validated risk scores.
