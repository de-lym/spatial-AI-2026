    About & limits
      
    Fictional, Manhattan-inspired scene: a “SoHo-like” zone with lower buildings (south) and a “Midtown-like” zone with taller, denser buildings (north). All buildings, street names, heights, zones and numbers are synthetic. Coordinates: local Cartesian x east, y north, z up in metres above one shared ground datum; 1 scene unit = 5 m. Obstacle checks are a simplified classroom rule, not certified collision avoidance. Regulatory inspiration (categories only, no compliance claimed): China's Interim Regulations on the Flight Management of Unmanned Aircraft (2024), CAAC CCAR-92 (2024), Shenzhen Low-Altitude Economy Regulations (2024), T/CSAA 21-2022. Real New York operations would need separate FAA and local-rule verification.
